Platform Capabilities

Everything Educational Institutions Need

From test design to instant credentialing, ExamPro delivers an end-to-end examination ecosystem.

1. Online Examinations

Distraction-free assessment interface with dynamic question palette, countdown timer, auto-saving drafts, and multi-format questions.

2. Question Bank

Centralized repository organized by subject, topic, and difficulty. Supports single MCQ, multi-select, true/false, fill-in-blanks, and CSV bulk import.

3. Real-Time Results

Instant scorecard generation with circular percentage score gauges, question-by-question review, explanations, and downloadable ReportLab PDF cards.

4. Performance Analytics

Interactive Chart.js visualizations tracking score progression over time, topic strengths and weaknesses, accuracy rates, and time per question.

5. Secure Examination

Enforced fullscreen proctoring, tab-switch and window-blur detection, copy-paste blocking, question randomization, and audit security logs.

6. Automated Evaluation

Server-side calculation including configurable negative marking penalty, grade assignment, and verified digital certificates of achievement.
